GOOD TO KNOW

Before you go

Weather

Early September in Abu Dhabi is hot and humid. Wear light fabrics, sunscreen and drink plenty of water.

Pack for the parks

Swimwear, reef shoes and a change of clothes for Yas Waterworld. Bring a light layer for indoor parks.

Mosque dress code

Shoulders, arms and legs covered. Women should bring a scarf for their hair.

Confirm before you land

Double-check which theme parks are included with the hotel's park pass.
