Useful Tips

✈ Airport

Arrive early for the outbound flight and allow enough time for security and airport procedures.

🎟 Attractions

Keep tickets and reservations accessible on your phone and check attraction opening times before leaving.

☀ Weather

September in Abu Dhabi is very hot. Carry water, sunscreen and comfortable clothing.

👟 Comfort

Wear comfortable walking shoes, especially on Warner Bros. World, Yas Waterworld and SeaWorld days.

🕌 Mosque

Dress respectfully when visiting Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque and follow the site's visitor guidelines.

🛍 Shopping

Yas Mall is planned for Day 2 evening after the waterpark and dinner.
